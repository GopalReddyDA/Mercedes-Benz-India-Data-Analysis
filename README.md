![Mercedes-Benz Cars Analysis](https://github.com/GopalReddyDA/Mercedes-Benz-India-Data-Analysis/blob/main/Mercedes-Benz-Logo.avif)
### Project Overview :

This project analyzes the Mercedes-Benz India model portfolio using Python.
The goal is to understand the company's vehicle portfolio from a business point of view  including:

Which body types have the most models?
How are models distributed across price ranges?
What is the average starting price?
How strong is the electric vehicle portfolio?
How is the SUV portfolio positioned?
Where are the major price gaps?
Which segments have more or fewer models?

The analysis uses Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly to turn the raw dataset into useful business insights.

Business Objective

The main objective is to understand:
How is the Mercedes-Benz India portfolio positioned across vehicle types, powertrains, and price segments?

📂 Dataset

Main columns
Column	Description

### Key Business Insights :
Some important findings from the analysis:
SUV is the largest portfolio category
SUVs represent the largest body-type group in the dataset.
This shows the importance of SUVs within the Mercedes-Benz India portfolio.

### Wide price range :
The portfolio starts around ₹51.8 lakh and extends to around ₹4.3 crore.
This gives Mercedes-Benz a wide price ladder covering premium customers through ultra-luxury customers.

### EV portfolio is growing :
Electric models are present across multiple body types and price points.
This shows that EVs are not limited to only one part of the luxury portfolio.

### Strong luxury positioning :
A significant portion of the portfolio is positioned above ₹1 crore.
This indicates a strong focus on the premium and luxury customer segments.

### Price gaps :
Some large gaps exist between consecutive models in the price ladder.
These gaps can be useful for understanding potential portfolio opportunities or areas where the brand has limited model coverage.

### SUV price coverage :
The SUV portfolio includes models below ₹1 crore as well as high-end SUVs above ₹2 crore.
This creates multiple SUV price levels for different customer segments.
